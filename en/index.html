Why here

What's waiting for you

Walls that breathe

The house is built from hempcrete with earth plaster. The walls insulate and balance humidity, and you feel it the moment you walk in.

A minute to the wadi

Cross the road and you're at the cliff edge. A trail leads down into Wadi Tzukim for a sunrise walk or the hour before sunset.

A pool in the desert

One of the few places around with a pool. A swim after a hot day changes everything.

The artists' village

Galleries, a coffee roastery, Indian food and pizza within walking distance. Thursday evenings and Friday mornings are liveliest.

Dark skies

Tzukim keeps its nights dark. Turn off the yard lights and watch the Milky Way from the deck.

A basket from the Arava

We also run Shushka, Tzukim's natural food store. Order local fruit and vegetables ahead and they'll be waiting in your fridge.

Your hosts

Assaf, Hila, Eden, Dvir and Hodaya

We came to Tzukim in 2013 and lived in a caravan, back when the village was still debating whether to have street lights at all. We spent three years thinking about our house before we started building, and fell in love with hemp. When we finished, ours was the fourth hemp house in Israel.

Assaf built the desert cooling systems and the water filtration himself. Hila makes sure you know everything before you leave home. And for twelve years we've been running Shushka, the village's natural food store.

"We want you to feel at home, because that's how we like to travel."

When to come

Every season has its desert

The Arava is dry and warm, and nights are always cooler than days.

October–NovemberAutumn

Warm days, pleasant evenings outside, a sukkah in the yard at Sukkot.

December–FebruaryWinter

Sunny days for hiking, cold nights and especially clear skies.

March–AprilSpring

The wadis bloom after the rain. The best time for walking.

May–SeptemberSummer

Very hot. Walk at sunrise, pool and cooler by day, stars at night.

Where is it?

Tzukim is in the Central Arava, a little over an hour from Eilat and about X hours from Tel Aviv. Free parking by the house. We'll send exact directions before you arrive.

Is it good for kids?

Very. Board games, toys for little ones, a pool (with supervision) and a crib on request. In the studio, keep the 26 steps in mind.

Is there air conditioning?

The studio has a strong air conditioner. The big house uses a desert cooler, which cools gently without drying the air, plus central AC in the living room for the rare humid days.

Can I work from there?

Yes. There's Wi-Fi and a workspace in the big house.

Can we host an event?

Tzukim is a quiet village, so we don't host parties. Families and friends who come to rest are very welcome.

What about the kitchen?

We don't cook meat in the house. If you do, please use the glass dishes or disposables. The big house has no microwave; the studio does.
